## What are value bundles?

A value bundle is a set of everyday products packed and sold together for less per item than buying each one separately. At [PoundMart](/), our bundles come in two types:

- **Same-product multipacks**, such as three bottles of XHC Argan Oil Shampoo, where you're simply stocking up on something you already use.
- **Mixed bundles**, such as the Nice Smile 12 pack of three fruity flavours, where you get variety as well as a lower price per tube.

Either way, the idea is the same: things you use every day shouldn't cost a premium just because you buy them one at a time.

## Why PoundMart sells in bundles

### 1. A lower price per item

This is the big one. Packing and sending one parcel of twelve tubes costs less per tube than sending twelve separate parcels. Bundles let us pass that saving on, which is why, across most of our range, the price per item falls as the pack gets bigger.

### 2. Fewer trips and fewer orders

Toothpaste, shampoo and conditioner run out at the least convenient moments. A bundle turns lots of small, last-minute purchases into one planned one, and stops you paying convenience-shop prices in a pinch.

### 3. Variety without the extra cost

Our mixed bundles let a household try several flavours or scents without buying each one separately at single prices. More on that below.

### 4. Genuine products you can trust

A bundle is only a bargain if what's inside is the real thing. Every PoundMart bundle is packaged and quality-checked by our UK-based team, so you know exactly what you're getting. There's more on that promise below.

## When a bundle isn't the best buy

We'd rather you buy the right pack than the biggest one. A bundle is poor value if:

- **You won't use it up in time.** Toothpaste tubes usually carry an expiry date, so check the pack and use the oldest tube first. Our guide to [whether toothpaste expires](/blog/does-toothpaste-expire) explains what the date means.
- **You're trying something for the first time.** If you don't know whether your family will like a flavour, or your curls will like a conditioner, a smaller pack is a cheaper experiment.
- **The price per item isn't actually lower.** Not every multipack is discounted, ours included. At the time of writing, the Nice Smile Feelin' Grape pack of 2 was £14.90, exactly two singles at £7.45. That pack is about convenience, not saving. For a wider look, see [whether multipacks are cheaper](/blog/are-multipacks-cheaper).
- **You have nowhere to put it.** An overflowing stock cupboard leads to forgotten, doubled-up purchases.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are value bundles always cheaper?

Usually, but not always. Most of our bundles cost less per item than buying singles, but a few small multipacks are priced at exactly the single price multiplied up. The quickest check is to divide the pack price by the number of items and compare it with the single price.

### How long does a toothpaste bundle last?

That depends on how many people brush and how much toothpaste they use. Tubes usually carry an expiry date, so check the dates on the pack, use the oldest tube first and choose a bundle size you'll comfortably finish in time.

### Is the Nice Smile 12 pack suitable for children?

The Nice Smile range is described as fluoride toothpaste for kids and adults. The NHS recommends a smear of toothpaste with no less than 1,000ppm fluoride for children under 3, and a pea-sized amount with more than 1,000ppm for children aged 3 to 6, so check the fluoride level on the pack. Your dentist can advise if you're unsure.
