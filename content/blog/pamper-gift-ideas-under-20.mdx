## What makes a great pamper gift under £20

A budget gift works best when it ticks four boxes:

- **It gets used up.** Consumables like shampoo, conditioner and toothpaste get used rather than stored, so they don't add clutter.
- **It feels personal.** Matching the gift to someone's hair type or favourite scent shows you've thought about it.
- **It looks the part.** Good presentation matters more than the price tag.
- **It's easy to exchange.** Buying from a retailer with a clear returns policy takes the risk out of guessing.

## Wrapping it up on a budget

Presentation turns a practical gift into a pamper gift. None of these ideas cost much:

1. **Reuse a sturdy box** (a shoebox works) and line it with tissue paper or shredded brown paper.
2. **Group items by colour** so the hamper looks planned.
3. **Tie it with twine or ribbon** and add a sprig of rosemary or lavender from the garden.
4. **Write a proper card.** A few lines about why you chose each item makes the gift feel personal.
